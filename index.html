FOUNDER & CHIEF ENGINEER

Janath & The Velocity Creed

Velocity Motors was founded by Janath, a visionary automotive engineer, engine tuner, and motorcycle racer. Driven by a passion for high-compression engines, aerodynamics, and precise exhaust tuning, Janath built Velocity to bridge the gap between track-grade spares and everyday commuters.

We source authentic motorcycle spares, high-performance chains, and custom-tuned exhausts directly from OEM partners. Under Janath's guidance, every single component is dyno-tested and calibrated for India's demanding terrains, ensuring your KTM, R15, Classic, or Pulsar operates at absolute peak output.

Dyno-Tested Guarantee

All piston kits and drive systems are certified for thermal and mechanical stress tolerances under Janath's supervision.

Precision Engine Blueprinting

Every single replacement engine kit is blueprinted to exact micron clearances before delivery.
